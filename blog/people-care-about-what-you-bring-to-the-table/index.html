People Care About What You Bring to the Table

There’s a hard truth you run into out here.

Actually… you run into it everywhere.

People don’t really care about you.

They care about what you bring to the table.

What you can offer.

What you can give.

What you can provide.

And when that runs out…

So does their interest.

There are exceptions.

People with a real moral compass.

People who help because they want to—

not because they gain something from it.

But they’re rare.

Far and few between.

You notice it more out here.

Because everything is stripped down.

There’s no hiding behind status.

No distractions.

No illusion of stability.

Just people.

And what they choose to do

when there’s nothing to gain.
