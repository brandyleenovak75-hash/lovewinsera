I’m Just Walking

Sun, 05 Apr 2026

He didn’t leave with a perfect plan.

He sold what he could, packed what he had left, and started walking north on the Appalachian Trail.

No clear destination. No guarantees. Just movement.

Carrying everything he owns on his back, he’s learning in real time what it means to keep going when there’s nowhere else to stand still.

The weight is real. The weather is unpredictable. The unknown is constant.

And still—he walks.
